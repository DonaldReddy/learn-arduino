# Buzzer

**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Concepts:** `tone()`/`noTone()`, frequency ↔ pitch, arrays for sequencing, piezoelectric effect

Plays the opening phrase of "Twinkle Twinkle Little Star" through a piezo
buzzer.

## Wiring
- Buzzer positive leg → pin 8
- Buzzer negative leg → GND
- No resistor needed — piezo elements draw negligible current

## What I learned
- **`tone(pin, frequency, duration)`** drives the pin with a square wave at
  a specific frequency (Hz). A piezo disc physically flexes at that
  frequency (reverse piezoelectric effect), pushing air at the same rate —
  heard as a musical pitch. Higher frequency = higher pitch.
- Arduino can only play **one tone at a time** — no chords — since it's
  generated by a single hardware timer.
- Needed a small delay *longer* than each note's duration between notes,
  otherwise two consecutive notes of the same pitch (like the opening two
  C4s) blur into one continuous tone instead of sounding like two distinct
  notes.
- Two buzzer types exist: **passive** (bare piezo disc, needs `tone()` to
  produce different pitches — what this project assumes) vs. **active**
  (potted unit with a built-in oscillator, only ever makes one fixed tone
  regardless of frequency — just needs `digitalWrite(pin, HIGH/LOW)`).
